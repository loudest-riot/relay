# nøfuture / Loudest Riot Sounds — Spotify Canvas Standards

This is the art direction for ALL future Canvas exports.

1. **Absolutely no visible words in Canvas frames.** Do not show track titles, artist name, album title, cover typography, captions, watermarks, dates, or fake branding. Spotify provides track details outside the video.
2. **Official materials only.** Start from original Hines Park photos, field imagery, and original artwork; never invent a replacement artist logo or Signal Glyph.
3. **Text-free fragments of cover artwork may be integrated**, with cropping or masking verified BEFORE render. Never drop an intact lettered square cover into the vertical frame.
4. **Hines Park is the protagonist:** reflective water, dark trees, dawn/dusk skies, real colors, unposed environmental details.
5. **Contrasty, not drab.** Preserve highlights, indigo/cobalt shadows, red-orange sunset accents, uncanny light, and a restrained camp-horror edge when appropriate.
6. **Real motion, not fake spectacle:** slow parallax, subtle movement in water, cloud drift, faint celestial effects. Avoid visual clutter.
7. **No visible editing artifacts:** no black bars, glitchy or duplicated letters, faded-out titles, poster cards, or accidental overlays.
8. **Spotify export:** 9:16 vertical MP4, H.264, 3–8 seconds, silent, seamless loop, no text. Check first, middle, and final frames.
9. **Audio remains its own work.** Canvas is a visual companion, not a lyric video, ad, or music video squeezed into seven seconds.
10. **Changes to one release must not overwrite other titles or shared branding assets.**

## Slight Chance of Misery (approved direction)
- Dark, threatening, but colorful Hines Park water and trees
- Distressed storm texture harvested from right-hand section of official release artwork
- Faint stars and reflected light; slow, subtle movement
- Absolutely **zero** visible artist or release lettering
- Master source renderer: `canvas/make_slight_chance.py`
- Output: `canvas/export/slight-chance-of-misery-canvas.mp4`

Maintain and reuse these guidelines for every future nøfuture Spotify Canvas.

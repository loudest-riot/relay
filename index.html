The artist / sound & place

nøfuture

Jennifer A. Paull is a Michigan-based artist working as nøfuture and releasing through Loudest Riot Sounds. Her current project, Hines Park Transmissions, connects sound composition with landscape, movement, and psychogeography.

She is also developing DRIFT, a companion project that invites exploration of place. Paull co-founded Public Pool gallery in Hamtramck. Her practice brings together sound, visual material, and a sustained interest in how people experience their surroundings.
